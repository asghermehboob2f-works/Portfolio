Professional Summary
I'm Asgher Mehboob, a Computer Science and Engineering student at Central University of Kashmir, web developer, and digital creator based in Jammu and Kashmir, India. I am passionate about turning ideas and rough drafts into functional digital products — from responsive full-stack websites to AI-integrated applications. Key projects include RUHGEN, an AI-powered platform for generating images and videos with streamlined creative workflows, and ISLAH, a civic reporting concept designed to empower people to report public issues such as waste accumulation, damaged roads, and streetlights. Through continuous project building, modern web technologies, and design thinking, I bridge technology and creative media to build useful, polished digital experiences.
